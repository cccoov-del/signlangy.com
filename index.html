How a lesson goes

Five minutes, three little steps.

Lessons are short on purpose. Learn a few signs, use them straight away, then put them together.

1

Watch

See Maya sign it, slow it down, and read how the hand moves.

2

Practice

Pick the right sign, tell look-alike signs apart, and try again when you miss.

3

Put it together

Build little sentences in ASL order and follow short everyday chats.

Quick reviews bring signs back just before you'd forget them. Pinky keeps track, so a few minutes a day is enough.

Which sign language does it teach?

American Sign Language (ASL), the sign language used in the United States and much of Canada. Sign languages differ by country, and signs can vary a little by region and person.

Will I be fluent after the course?

No, and we won't pretend otherwise. SignLangy teaches the basics: 45 everyday signs, fingerspelling and simple sentences. That's enough to get by in short, simple conversations, and a good start for learning more.

Who is Maya?

Maya is the AI teacher in every sign video. Her movements are based on open recordings of real people signing. If a sign ever looks wrong, tap "Report a problem" in the app and we'll look at it.

Does it need the internet?

No. All the lessons and videos come with the app, so it works offline, on a plane or anywhere else.

Does the camera watch me sign?

Camera practice works like a mirror, so you can see your own hands next to the sign. It doesn't record, save or send anything, and it doesn't grade you.

Do I need an account?

No. Just open it and start. Your progress is saved on your phone. See the privacy policy.

Is it on iPhone?

SignLangy is coming to Google Play for Android first.

What if I want a refund?

Refunds go through Google Play under its usual rules. If something isn't working, email us first and we'll try to help.

Spotted a wrong sign?

Or got a question, or an idea? We read every email, and fixes go into the next update.
