Privacy policy

SignLangy: Intro to ASL. Last updated 12 November 2026.

Short version: SignLangy works on your phone. It has no accounts, no ads and no tracking, and it does not send your information to us or to anyone else.

What the app stores on your phone

  • The name and age range you type when you start. They are used only to greet you and to set up your plan.
  • Your learning progress: lessons finished, review schedule, scores, streaks and badges.
  • Your settings, such as language, theme, reminders and favourite signs.

This information stays on your device. We cannot see it.

What we collect

Nothing. This version of SignLangy does not collect personal information, does not use analytics, and does not share data with third parties.

Camera

Camera practice is optional. If you turn it on, the camera works as a mirror so you can watch your own hands. The picture is shown on your screen only. It is not recorded, saved or sent anywhere.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, they are scheduled on your phone. No notification service of ours is involved.

Purchases

SignLangy is a paid download. Payment is handled by Google Play (or the App Store). We do not receive your card or payment details.

Children

SignLangy does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your choices

  • See your data: Profile → Download my data makes a file with everything the app stores about you.
  • Delete your data: Profile → Delete my account and data erases it. Uninstalling the app also removes it.

Changes

If a later version of SignLangy works differently, for example by adding optional accounts, we will update this page before that version is released.

Contact

Write to support@signlangy.com.
